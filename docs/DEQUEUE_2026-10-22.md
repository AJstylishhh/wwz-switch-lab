# Dequeue evidence — October 22, 2026

The Switch reaches a third RPC after our experimental EnvironmentList and Enqueue responses:

`/0ae806e0260340fe86eb38ff8708252f.svc/c4504538d0ca4b2fb2a9e87f0091f97e`

Observed request length: 813 bytes. Our issued token is 51 bytes (53 including protobuf framing), and the client advertises 19 service versions of 40 encoded bytes each: 53 + 19×40 = **813**. This very strongly identifies the RPC as `IAuthorizationService_Dequeue` (field 1 token, repeated field 2 ServiceVersionRecord). Its descriptor-defined response is `ServiceResult` with field **323326796** `ServiceResult_SignInResult`, containing a `SignInResult` with tokens, UserId and service endpoints.

The response serializer has been checked offline against the actual binary protobuf descriptors, but a Switch has NOT confirmed acceptance. A successful HTTP 200 or schema decode is not yet a functioning WWZ login or gameplay session. The main-menu WWZ connection warning may persist until later authorization, presence and heartbeat calls succeed; it does not yet establish the need for a separate online-status API.

P2P remains the priority: complete authorization, player presence, lobby discovery and host negotiation before integrating the standalone UDP forwarding lab. Never upload raw captured credentials or console login data to this public repository.
